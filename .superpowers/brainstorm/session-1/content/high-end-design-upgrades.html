5 Core Agency Design Directives Implemented in Code:

1. Double-Bezel Architecture (Doppelrand)

Every card is engineered like physical Apple hardware: an outer protective tray (`p-2`, `rounded-[1.5rem]`, translucent ring) housing an inner machined core (`rounded-[1.25rem]`, inner highlight).

2. Nested "Button-in-Button" CTAs

Action buttons feature flush circular icon capsules (`w-6 h-6 rounded-full`). On hover, the capsule translates diagonally (`group-hover:translate-x-0.5 group-hover:-translate-y-0.5`) with kinetic tension.

3. Analog Tactile Noise Tooth

A fixed, non-scrolling SVG noise texture overlay (`opacity: 0.025`, pointer-events-none) eliminates digital sterile plastic vibes and gives the dark theme a physical, publication-grade feel.

4. Geometric Grotesk Typography Rhythm

Strict sans typography (`Geist` + `Geist Mono`), tight letter-spacing (`tracking-[-0.03em]`), and micro pill eyebrow tags with `tracking-[0.2em]`.
