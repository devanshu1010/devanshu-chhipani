Why this blows visitors away while staying subtle & sophisticated:

1. Natural Narrative Flow: You aren't forcing the visitor to read a boring resume. The entire website reads like an architectural walkthrough of a high-end system.

2. Interactive High-Level Design (HLD) in Stage 3: Rather than flat screenshots or generic cards, the centerpiece of both modes is a living, interactive HLD schematic where visitors can click on the stages (Embeddings ➔ Vector DB ➔ Reranker in AI, or Gateway ➔ DAG ➔ Postgres in SE) to inspect latency, data schemas, and design tradeoffs.

3. Two Perspectives, Same Engineering Rigor: Recruiter or VP looking for an AI engineer sees an end-to-end AI researcher who understands vectors and inference pipelines. Looking for a Senior Backend engineer, they toggle and see a hardened distributed systems architect who understands DAGs, transactions, and p99 SLAs.
